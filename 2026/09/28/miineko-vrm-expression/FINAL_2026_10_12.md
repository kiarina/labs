## Checked scope and limits

The production handoff reports zero ribbon wing/tail interference against the
whole body and zero ribbon self-intersection over 12 ordinary poses. The knot
has intentional chest contact, with a fixed distance of 3.5 mm. Twelve
face expressions were retained. Blender reload and reproduction matched 167
arrays. These checks describe their tested scope; they do not establish
unrestricted motion safety, complete source-FBX regeneration or validation in
every target runtime.

Upper and rear-oblique gaps remain. Contact under strong downward head
bending was observed on an earlier version. K has not been rechecked under that condition, so its
behavior there remains unverified and is not guaranteed. The owner accepted
the comparison with these limits and finished the K delivery. Mouth/speech polish, general generation research,
gaze and spring integration retain their separate scopes.
