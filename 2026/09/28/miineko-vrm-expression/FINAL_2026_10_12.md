## Checked scope and limits

The production handoff reports zero whole-body interference and zero ribbon
self-intersection over 12 ordinary poses, with the ribbon center fixed. Twelve
face expressions were retained. Blender reload and reproduction matched 167
arrays. These checks describe their tested scope; they do not establish
unrestricted motion safety, complete source-FBX regeneration or validation in
every target runtime.

Upper and rear-oblique gaps remain. Strong downward head bending can still
cause interference. The owner accepted the comparison with these limits and
finished the K delivery. Mouth/speech polish, general generation research,
